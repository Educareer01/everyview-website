THE EVERYVIEW IDEA

One place for the cameras that matter.

EveryView is a mobile app in development for households and businesses using multiple security cameras or video doorbells. The goal is a simpler dashboard for compatible devices, without pretending every camera works the same way.

1

Add compatible cameras
Connect supported devices using authorised, supported methods.

2

See them together
Bring live views and camera access into a consistent dashboard.

3

Stay in control
Clear privacy choices and security-first design are part of the product direction.

COMPATIBILITY

Clear about what works — and what doesn't.

EveryView is being designed to support compatible cameras through open standards and authorised integrations where available. Compatibility will vary by manufacturer, model and enabled features. We won't describe a device as supported until we've verified it.
