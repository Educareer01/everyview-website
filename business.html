COMPATIBILITY MATTERS

Built around the systems businesses already use.

Business cameras may connect through supported local standards, network video recorders or authorized manufacturer integrations. Support varies by device, model, network configuration and permissions. EveryView is researching both consumer and professional camera systems; no manufacturer integration is guaranteed yet.

Security and access come first. Home and workplace views must remain appropriately separated, with permission-based access for authorized users. Some camera services may require their own subscriptions, which would be disclosed before connection.
