Privacy Notice

Publication draft — September 2026.

EveryView is operated by EveryView, 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. This page is being prepared as the public privacy notice for the EveryView service.

What EveryView is

EveryView is a mobile security-camera application in development. It is intended to help users bring compatible security cameras and video doorbells together in one place.

Camera credentials and connections

Where credentials are required for an authorised camera connection, the application is designed to encrypt those credentials at rest and decrypt them only when needed to establish the authorised connection. Credentials should not be returned to the user interface, displayed in plaintext or intentionally logged.

AI features

AI-assisted camera analysis is intended to be optional and off by default. When a user chooses to enable an AI feature that requires external processing, a relevant snapshot may be sent to an external AI provider through EveryView's service infrastructure. The production provider route and applicable contractual terms must be verified before public launch.

Research and contact details

If you participate in EveryView research, survey answers and contact details should be collected only for the stated purposes. Marketing permission should be separate and optional.

Your rights

UK data-protection law may give you rights over your personal information, including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability in applicable circumstances.

Contact

Privacy enquiries: privacy@every-view.co.uk

This notice remains a pre-launch draft and must be reviewed against the live production data flows, processors, retention periods and international transfers before the service is publicly launched.
