Market Research Privacy Notice

Effective 28 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Who is responsible

EVERYVIEW LTD (company number 17484891) is the data controller. Registered office and correspondence address: 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. Contact: privacy@every-view.co.uk.

Scope

This notice covers our market research questionnaire, optional complimentary-storage registration and separate marketing opt-in. It does not cover the future EveryView camera app, which will have a separate notice before launch.

What we collect

We collect questionnaire answers about camera brands, numbers and locations of devices or business premises, experiences, preferences, pricing and features, plus voluntary free-text answers. We collect email addresses where provided for the launch offer, and record any separate marketing choice. Campaign tracking fields (UTM source, medium, campaign, content and survey version) may be passed in survey links. Tally may process technical data such as IP address, device/browser details and submission timestamps for its service.

Why and lawful basis

We use answers for market research, product planning and aggregated analysis, relying on our legitimate interests under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f). We use offer email addresses to administer the complimentary-storage registration under legitimate interests. We send optional promotional email only where separately opted in, relying on consent under Article 6(1)(a) and applicable electronic marketing rules. You may object to legitimate-interests processing and withdraw marketing consent.

Storage and sharing

Responses are collected and stored through Tally, which states that form data is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in Europe. Tally acts as our data processor under its data-processing agreement. Authorised EveryView personnel can access responses in Tally. If we enable Google Workspace, Sheets, email notifications or exports, relevant data may also be held there. We do not sell identifiable responses. We may share anonymous aggregated findings with investors and partners; service providers and advisers may process data for us under appropriate arrangements.

International transfers

Tally is based in Belgium and stores form data in Europe. If service-provider access or other processing involves restricted transfers outside the UK, we will use an applicable adequacy arrangement or lawful transfer safeguards.

Retention

Identifiable research responses will be deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 12 months after the campaign closes, subject to lawful exceptions. Anonymous aggregate findings may be retained longer. Offer-registration details will be retained until six calendar months after the official service launch, plus up to 90 days for claims administration, then deleted unless another lawful reason applies. If the service has not launched within 24 months after the campaign closes, we will delete offer-registration details unless participants actively renew. Marketing details are kept until opt-out or no longer needed, with inactive contacts reviewed at least every 24 months; limited suppression records may be retained to respect opt-outs. Exports and integrations will follow these periods, subject to backup deletion cycles.

Three months of complimentary storage

Eligible participants who provide an email address may claim three months of complimentary storage if and when EveryView launches. Claims must be made within six calendar months after the official launch date announced by EveryView. The complimentary period begins on successful activation. Eligibility, compatible equipment and offer terms will be published before redemption. Launch and availability are not guaranteed. Registration for the offer is not consent to marketing.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. Marketing is optional and not preselected. Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. Contact privacy@every-view.co.uk. Anonymous responses may be impossible to locate individually. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Security and participation

We limit access to authorised personnel and use reasonable account and data security measures. The survey is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Do not submit camera credentials, footage, precise security details or sensitive personal information.

Changes

We may update this notice if our research arrangements change. This page shows the current version. For correspondence: EVERYVIEW LTD, 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom.
